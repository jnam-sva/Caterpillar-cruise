# Caterpillar Cruise

Steer a caterpillar with an invisible steering wheel — your arms, tracked by your webcam (MediaPipe Pose). Pick a music theme, follow the beat, and reach your tent before time runs out.

**Play:** https://jnam-sva.github.io/Caterpillar-cruise/

- Use Chrome on a laptop with a webcam, and allow camera access.
- Stand back so both arms are in frame, then hold your arms like a steering wheel.
